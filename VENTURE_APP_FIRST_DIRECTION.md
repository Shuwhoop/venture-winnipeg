# Venture App-First Direction

Venture is an app first. The web address is only the delivery, installation, and deep-link layer. It is not a separate website experience.

## Product rules

- The default experience is a persistent app shell.
- Today, Explore, Build, Saved, and Profile are the primary destinations.
- Bottom navigation is the main navigation.
- Public marketing footers do not appear inside the app shell.
- Browsing and building remain available in Guest mode.
- Accounts are optional and exist for sync, recovery, preferences, and notifications.
- Mobile layouts are designed first, then expanded carefully for larger screens.
- Installation, offline behaviour, deep links, native sharing, calendar actions, and notifications are treated as core product features.
- A future public marketing page may live on a separate route or domain, but it must never replace the app as Venture's main experience.
