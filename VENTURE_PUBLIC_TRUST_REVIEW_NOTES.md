# Venture Public Trust Foundation

This package adds plain-language starter pages for:

- Privacy
- Terms of Use
- Accessibility
- A compact public footer linking to all three pages

## Before a broad public launch

1. Set `NEXT_PUBLIC_VENTURE_CONTACT_EMAIL` in Vercel and local development.
2. Have a qualified Canadian lawyer review the Terms and Privacy language.
3. Confirm that the written retention and data-handling descriptions match production operations.
4. Complete keyboard, screen-reader, zoom, contrast, reduced-motion, and mobile testing.
5. Keep a record of newsletter consent and a functioning unsubscribe process.

## Official references reviewed

- Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada: PIPEDA requirements and fair information principles
- CRTC: CASL consent, identification, and unsubscribe requirements
- Accessibility Manitoba: Accessible Customer Service and Information and Communication standards

This package is a practical product foundation, not a substitute for legal advice.
