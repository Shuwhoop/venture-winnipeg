# Venture Small Beta Test Plan

## First beta size
- 10 to 20 testers
- Mostly Winnipeg students and young adults
- Include at least two couples, two newcomers, two frequent event-goers, and two people who rarely plan outings
- Test on a mix of iPhone, Android, and desktop browsers

## Testing window
Run the first round for five to seven days. Repair critical problems during the test, but avoid changing the entire interface halfway through the round.

## Success signals
- Testers can find a useful listing quickly
- Testers understand verification and source information
- Most testers can finish the outing builder without help
- Partial results explain what is missing
- Save and share workflows work on real phones
- Feedback identifies repeatable problems rather than isolated preferences

## Stop-the-launch problems
- Incorrect event dates or opening hours
- Broken directions, phone, website, reservation, menu, or ticket actions
- Outings that appear complete but contain closed or impossible stops
- Horizontal overflow or blocked actions on common phone widths
- Feedback, claims, or reports failing to save
- Private/admin information appearing publicly

## After the round
1. Group feedback into bugs, confusion, trust/data, and ideas.
2. Fix repeated blockers first.
3. Export fresh analytics.
4. Compare completion, save, share, replacement, and feedback rates.
5. Run a second smaller round before a public launch.
