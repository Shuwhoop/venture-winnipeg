# Venture Decision Log

## Locked decisions

- Founder: Prince Malimbiza
- Launch city: Winnipeg, citywide
- Primary users: students and young adults aged roughly 18–30
- Secondary users: couples planning dates and newcomers exploring Winnipeg
- Events and food are equal launch pillars
- Core promise: find a trustworthy event, restaurant, or complete outing in under two minutes
- Brand: Venture
- Working tagline: Discover Your City.
- AI remains behind the scenes and is not the public identity
- Quality, accuracy, source traceability, legality, and freshness outrank raw listing volume
- Guests can browse without an account
- Basic preferences may be stored locally; synced personalization requires an account and consent
- Unverified listings may appear with a clear warning and lower ranking
- Businesses can claim listings for free after ownership verification
- Basic corrections and accuracy are never paywalled
- No traditional open-ended public reviews at launch
- Venture uses structured feedback and may show separately attributed external ratings where legally permitted
- One weekly newsletter at launch: Venture This Weekend, sent Thursday
- First technical product: mobile-first web app with PWA support
- Native apps come only after recurring usage is proven
- Approved visual baseline: clean light interface, purple accent, five-tab navigation, scannable cards, visible trust information

## Technical recommendation awaiting founder lock

- Next.js + TypeScript on Vercel
- Supabase/PostgreSQL + PostGIS
- MapLibre with licensed tiles
- Google Places UI Kit only as optional, separated external content
- Resend for newsletter delivery after compliance infrastructure is ready
