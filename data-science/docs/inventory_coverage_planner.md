# Inventory Coverage Planner

## Purpose

The Inventory Coverage Planner converts Venture's listing-quality findings into a practical collection and repair plan. It helps answer one operational question:

> Which listings should Venture repair or collect next so users receive more complete and varied outings?

## What it measures

- trusted published places
- trusted future events
- outing-eligible and pitch-ready records
- missing neighbourhood information
- trusted supply by neighbourhood
- trusted supply by category
- category-by-neighbourhood coverage
- future event supply by week
- drafts with the highest repair value

## How the priority score works

The draft priority score is a transparent checklist rather than a machine-learning model. Serious missing fields reduce the score. Existing verification, a known neighbourhood, and the ability to fill a thin category or neighbourhood increase it.

The score is intended to order work, not predict commercial success.

## Connection to Venture's mission

Venture promises trustworthy city discovery and complete outings. Sparse or incomplete inventory limits that promise even when the recommendation logic works correctly. The planner therefore connects database maintenance directly to user value:

1. repair trustworthy listings
2. improve neighbourhood and category depth
3. increase the outing builder's candidate pool
4. produce more complete and varied plans
5. test recommendations using stronger evidence

## Data-analysis skills demonstrated

- dimensional coverage analysis
- completeness and readiness metrics
- fixed-target progress tracking
- transparent weighted prioritization
- category and neighbourhood segmentation
- operational reporting
- translating analysis into product action

## Honest limitation

The initial targets are operating targets, not statistically estimated demand forecasts. They provide enough choice for a fair beta test and should be revised after Venture has meaningful user behaviour and partner feedback.
