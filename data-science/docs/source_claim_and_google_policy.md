# Venture Source, Claim, and Mapping Policy

## Google is a compass, not Venture's warehouse

Venture may use a mapping service to help a visitor navigate to a listing or to help an administrator locate an official source. Venture should not treat third-party ratings, reviews, photographs, or descriptions as Venture-owned inventory.

Public listing facts should come from official business, venue, organizer, municipal, or otherwise traceable sources. Mapping links remain clearly separate from Venture's own listing content.

## Business claims

Claiming a listing is free. A claim request verifies the relationship between a person and a business. It does not automatically publish edits, improve organic ranking, or purchase trust.

Administrators should verify claims using an official email domain, the listed business phone, the official website, or suitable documentation before approval.

## Public corrections

Incorrect-information reports are evidence for review, not automatic edits. Administrators should compare a report with trustworthy sources, update the listing where necessary, and record resolution notes.

## Product purpose

These rules support Venture's central promise: trustworthy Winnipeg discovery and complete outings built from current, traceable information.
