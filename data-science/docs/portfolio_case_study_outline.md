# Venture Data Portfolio Case Study Outline

## 1. Product problem

Winnipeg information is fragmented across event pages, restaurant directories, social media, and search results. Venture aims to turn trustworthy local inventory into a complete outing in under two minutes.

## 2. Data problem

The system must balance inventory freshness, source rights, schedule compatibility, geographic constraints, user preferences, and sparse early-stage behavioural data.

## 3. Data architecture

Explain Supabase/PostgreSQL, PostGIS, listings, sources, verifications, analytics events, feedback, experiments, audit logs, and admin reporting.

## 4. Instrumentation

Show the controlled event taxonomy and anonymous session design.

## 5. Analysis

Include funnel drop-off, cohort retention, outing completion, inventory gaps, recommendation-version performance, and feedback signals.

## 6. Experiment

Describe the outing-results presentation experiment, its hypothesis, primary metric, guardrails, confidence intervals, and minimum sample rule.

## 7. Recommendation evaluation

Compare complete-route rate, saves, shares, replacements, feedback, candidate count, and diversity.

## 8. Decision

Explain one product or inventory decision supported by the evidence.

## 9. Limitations

Discuss sample size, self-selection, instrumentation changes, inventory sparsity, and proxy metrics.

## 10. Skills demonstrated

SQL, Python, pandas, data cleaning, product metrics, experimentation, cohort analysis, recommendation evaluation, data visualization, privacy, and communication.
