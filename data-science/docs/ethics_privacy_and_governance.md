# Ethics, Privacy, and Governance

Venture's data systems should help people discover Winnipeg without turning ordinary city exploration into surveillance.

## Current principles

- no raw IP addresses in the analytics model
- no precise user-location history
- no keystroke recording
- no advertising identifier matching
- anonymous visitor and session IDs
- direct browser access to analytics tables is blocked
- admin APIs require authorized accounts
- contact email is used only with explicit permission
- source rights and verification remain traceable
- sponsored placement cannot secretly alter organic ranking

## Analyst responsibilities

- aggregate before sharing results
- redact contact information from analysis files
- separate synthetic practice data from real evidence
- state sample sizes and uncertainty
- document metric definitions before comparing results
- keep raw exports out of Git
- delete unnecessary local exports after completing an analysis
