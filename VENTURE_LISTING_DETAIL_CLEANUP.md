- Uses Venture's cream, yellow, red, pink, green, orange, and ink palette across the entire page.

- Removes the duplicate neighbourhood summary card because the hero already shows location.
- Removes the repetitive Why Venture Picked It section.
- Prevents the full description from repeating the short description.

- Combines the map, transit, parking, and accessibility into one Getting There section.
- Combines category, atmosphere, dietary, audience, and experience tags into one What to Expect section.
- Keeps source and freshness details in one compact section near the bottom.
- Retains directions, booking, tickets, visit history, phone, website, menu, email, related listings, reporting, and business claims.

No Supabase migration is required.
