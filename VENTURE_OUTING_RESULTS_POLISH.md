# Venture Outing Results Polish v0.2

Venture remains an app-first product. This pass improves the generated outing experience without turning the screen into a marketing page.

## Changes

- Replaces the save and share artwork with cleaner app-standard icons.
- Rebuilds the quick-action shelf so Save, Share, and Refresh read as one system.
- Simplifies the outing snapshot into a calm dashboard with consistent cards.
- Rewrites the additional-stop section and prevents its text from collapsing into icon-sized boxes.
- Removes the repetitive recommendation statement from generated, saved, and shared outing views.
- Adds staggered route-card entrances, section reveals, bookmark feedback, route-line movement, and detail expansion motion.
- Keeps reduced-motion support for users who disable animation.
- Uses a tighter cream, ink, yellow, red, purple, and green system instead of unrelated gradients competing for attention.

## Product direction

The outing result should feel like a small city cockpit: clear first, lively second. Motion explains progression. It should not make every card shout at once.
